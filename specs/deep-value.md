# DEEP_VALUE lens

Use for washed-out names near major support/value, capitulation reversals, and early mean-reversion entries.

## Criteria

### C1 — Discount to support / value (0-2)
Is price materially discounted into a major support zone, prior demand area, or lower value region?
- 2 = clear flush into major support or deep value zone
- 1 = somewhat discounted, but not yet at an obvious reset area
- 0 = not meaningfully discounted or still too elevated

### C2 — Capitulation / exhaustion (0-2)
Does the chart show signs of seller exhaustion or capitulation?
- 2 = flush, panic volume, wide-range rejection, or clear exhaustion behavior
- 1 = some stress is visible, but not decisive
- 0 = decline still looks orderly and unfinished

### C3 — Momentum reversal attempt (0-2)
Are RSI/stochastic turning up from oversold or deeply weak territory?
- 2 = clear reversal attempt from oversold with follow-through
- 1 = early turn, but not confirmed
- 0 = still pinned weak or rolling over again

### C4 — Hold / reclaim quality (0-1)
Is price holding the flush low, reclaiming a key level, or recovering back into value?
- 1 = yes
- 0 = no

### C5 — Trend damage awareness (0-1)
Is the setup stabilizing despite trend damage, rather than accelerating lower?
- 1 = yes
- 0 = no

## Trigger Conditions

Treat the setup as actionable only when one of these is present:
- capitulation flush into major support followed by a strong rejection
- failed breakdown that quickly reclaims the lost level
- higher low after the panic low
- recovery back into the lower value area with hold
- oversold momentum turn plus visible demand response

## Score Interpretation

- 0–2 = Avoid: still a falling knife or no credible reversal evidence
- 3–4 = Watch: discount is improving, but the reversal is still incomplete
- 5–6 = Review: reversal evidence is building, but trend damage is still a real risk
- 7–8 = Actionable: washout has likely exhausted and a tradable reversal is underway

## Common Deep Value Patterns

- capitulation flush into major support
- failed breakdown reclaim
- double-bottom attempt after panic selling
- wide-range reversal off value support
- oversold bounce that starts to build a higher low

## Setup states

- washed out
- probing
- reversing
- triggered

## Entry/action zone language

Prefer one of:
- no entry
- watch / wait
- deep-value probe
- pilot only
- reversal trigger
- reclaim entry

## Invalidation

Use the nearest chart-based failure condition:
- break of the capitulation low
- failure back under the reclaimed support or value edge
- loss of the first higher low after reversal
- immediate rejection that confirms the bounce was only reflexive

## Context Awareness

When possible, consider:
- whether the support zone is major or only minor local structure
- whether the flush was capitulatory or just one more leg in an unfinished downtrend
- whether the bounce is only reflexive into overhead supply
- whether volume profile shows room back into value or heavy supply overhead

If context is unclear, assume neutral positioning and describe uncertainty explicitly.
