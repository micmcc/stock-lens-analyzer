# ACCUMULATION lens

Use for base-building, early positioning near support, tightening ranges, and pre-breakout watchlist ranking.

## Criteria

### C1 — Base structure (0-2)
Is price building a usable base, higher low, or support shelf rather than trending lower?
- 2 = clear base, higher low sequence, or support shelf is forming
- 1 = early stabilization, but structure is still loose
- 0 = still trending lower or repeatedly losing support

### C2 — Absorption / participation (0-2)
Does the chart show seller absorption or demand stepping in near support?
- 2 = repeated support holds, tight closes, and/or clear volume response off support
- 1 = some stabilization, but sponsorship is still modest
- 0 = weak response, persistent supply, or no evidence of absorption

### C3 — Momentum repair (0-2)
Are RSI/stochastic turning up from weak territory without already looking stretched?
- 2 = clear turn up from weak territory with follow-through
- 1 = early turn, but not confirmed
- 0 = still weak, rolling over, or already too extended for an accumulation entry

### C4 — Value / support location (0-1)
Is price sitting near a credible support zone, value node, or prior demand area?
- 1 = yes
- 0 = no

### C5 — Trend stabilization (0-1)
Is price holding the EMA, reclaiming it, or at least flattening beneath it?
- 1 = yes
- 0 = no

## Trigger Conditions

Treat the setup as actionable only when one of these is present:
- higher low or support shelf holds after a pullback
- reclaim of the EMA or top of the local value area with follow-through
- tight range near support followed by expansion
- washout into support followed by an orderly recovery
- volume response at support that proves demand is active

## Score Interpretation

- 0–2 = Avoid: no base, no support response, or still catching a falling setup
- 3–4 = Watch: stabilization is starting, but the base is still immature
- 5–6 = Review: accumulation evidence is building, but the trigger still needs respect
- 7–8 = Actionable: base is usable and demand is proving itself near support

## Common Accumulation Patterns

- support shelf with repeated holds
- higher low after washout
- tight base under local resistance
- value-area reclaim after a weak flush
- low-volatility compression near demand

## Setup states

- basing
- early
- tightening
- triggered

## Entry/action zone language

Prefer one of:
- no entry
- watch / wait
- support entry
- pilot only
- accumulation trigger
- reclaim entry

## Invalidation

Use the nearest chart-based failure condition:
- loss of the support shelf
- failure back under the reclaimed EMA or value edge
- break of the most recent higher low
- expansion lower after a weak base

## Context Awareness

When possible, consider:
- whether the base is fresh or already several rotations old
- whether this is first support test or repeated support fatigue
- whether overhead supply is still too close for clean reward/risk
- whether the move is quietly improving or already turning into a breakout extension

If context is unclear, assume neutral positioning and describe uncertainty explicitly.
