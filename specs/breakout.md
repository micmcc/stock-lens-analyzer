# BREAKOUT lens

Use for entry analysis, breakout quality checks, reclaim setups, and watchlist ranking.

## Criteria

### C1 — Structure break (0-2)
Has price broken above the recent lower-high sequence or consolidation ceiling and held the break?
- 2 = clear break or successful retest hold
- 1 = improving structure but not fully confirmed
- 0 = still below the relevant structure

### C2 — Participation (0-2)
Did the move show convincing participation?
- 2 = clear candle expansion and/or obvious volume expansion vs recent bars
- 1 = some improvement but not decisive
- 0 = weak participation

### C3 — Momentum shift (0-2)
Are RSI/stochastic turning constructive without looking exhausted?
- 2 = clear constructive turn
- 1 = early curl / partial confirmation
- 0 = no helpful momentum shift

### C4 — Acceptance vs supply (0-1)
Is price sitting above or reclaiming the top of the nearby value area / overhead supply zone?
- 1 = yes
- 0 = no

### C5 — Trend alignment (0-1)
Is price above the key EMA or reclaiming it with follow-through?
- 1 = yes
- 0 = no

## Setup states
- early
- triggered
- extended
- failing

## Entry/action zone language
Prefer one of:
- no entry
- watch / wait
- breakout trigger
- retest entry
- pilot only
- extended / wait for pullback

## Invalidation
Use the nearest chart-based failure condition:
- loss of reclaim
- loss of swing low
- failure back under breakout zone
